1. Nouns (Suna)

Bayanani: Noun shi ne duk wata kalma da ke zama suna na mutum, wuri, dabba, ko abu.

Nau'ikan Nouns:

  • Proper Noun: Sunan musamman (kamar: Lukman, Kano, Nigeria).
  • Common Noun: Suna na gaba-ɗaya (kamar: boy, city, book).
  • Countable Noun: Abubuwan da za a iya ƙirga su (kamar: car, pen).
  • Uncountable Noun: Ba za a iya ƙirga su ba (kamar: water, sugar).
Misalai a Jimla:
• Lukman lives in Kano.
• Bring the book on the table.

2. Pronouns (Wakilin Suna)

Pronoun shi ne kalmar da ake amfani da ita maimakon Noun don guje wa maimaita suna.

Rukunin Pronouns:
• I: Ni | He: Shi | She: Ita | They: Su | We: Mu

3. Verbs (Aikatau)

Verb shi ne zuciyar jimla, yana nuna aikin da aka yi ko yanayi.

Misali: She is reading a book.

4. Adjectives (Siffa)

Adjective yana siffanta Noun (girma, launi, ko kyau).

Misali: He bought a new car.

5. Adverbs (Bayanin Aikatau)

Adverb yana ba da ƙarin bayani game da aiki (yadda, yaushe, ko ina).

Misali: He runs fast.
