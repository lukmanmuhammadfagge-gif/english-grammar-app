Jerin Darussa (Lessons)

1. Nouns (Suna)

Danna don karanta cikakken bayanin sunaye da misalai.

2. Pronouns (Wakilin Suna)

Danna don karanta cikakken bayanin wakilin suna.

3. Verbs (Aiki/Afe-Afe)

Danna don karanta bayanin aikatau da nau'ikansa.

1. Nouns (Suna)

Ameen: Noun shi ne kalmar da ke wakiltar suna na mutum, wuri, dabba, ko abu.

Nau'ikan Nouns:

  • Proper Nouns: Sunayen na musamman (kamar: Kano, Lukman, Nigeria).
  • Common Nouns: Sunaye na gaba ɗaya (kamar: boy, city, school, book).
Misalai a Jimloli:
• Lukman is going to Kano.
• The book is on the table.

2. Pronouns (Wakilin Suna)

Pronoun shi ne kalmar da ake amfani da ita maimakon Noun don guje wa sake maimaita suna.

Misalai:
• He (Shine - na namiji)
• She (Ita - ta mace)
• They (Su - mutane da yawa)
• It (Shi/Ita - abu ko dabba)

3. Verbs (Aikatau)

Verb shi ne kalmar da ke nuna aiki, aikata wani abu, ko yanayi.

Misalai:
• Ali is running (Ali yana gudu).
• She reads a book (Tana karanta littafi).
